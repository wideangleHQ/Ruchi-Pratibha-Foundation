# Project Name

Ruchi Prativa Foundation Digital Platform

---

# Vision

To create the permanent digital home of Ruchi Prativa Foundation that preserves its legacy, showcases its impact, celebrates excellence, enables community participation, and continues serving society for the next several decades.

This platform should become the Foundation's official institutional identity on the internet.

---

# About The Foundation

Ruchi Prativa Foundation is a charitable foundation established in 1997 under the Ruchi Group.

For nearly three decades, the Foundation has worked across multiple areas including:

- Talent Recognition
- Education
- Healthcare
- Community Development
- Disaster Relief
- Cultural Preservation
- Literary Activities
- Publications
- Environmental Initiatives
- Youth Development
- CSR Activities
- Social Welfare

The Foundation's flagship initiative is **Ruchi Prativa Sanman**, an annual recognition platform that has honoured distinguished personalities from Odisha for over 27 years.

The Foundation also publishes institutional magazines including:

- Amaruchi
- Prativayana

These publications preserve the Foundation's history, philosophy, activities, CSR initiatives, leadership messages, cultural contributions, and institutional knowledge.

# Project Mission

The purpose of this platform is not merely to publish information.

It must become:

- The official website of the Foundation
- The institutional archive
- The digital heritage repository
- The CSR platform
- The volunteer platform
- The publication library
- The Hall of Fame
- The media centre
- The transparency portal
- The foundation's long-term digital identity

Every feature added to the platform should strengthen one or more of these objectives.

---

# Digital Philosophy

This platform should be designed as an institutional platform rather than a campaign website.

The focus is on:

- Legacy
- Trust
- Authenticity
- Transparency
- Community
- Documentation
- Knowledge Preservation
- Long-Term Scalability

Avoid common NGO website patterns that focus only on fundraising or campaigns.

The Foundation represents an institution with decades of history and should be presented accordingly.

# Definition of Success

This platform is successful when:

- It establishes trust with the public.
- It preserves institutional history.
- It showcases measurable impact.
- It celebrates excellence.
- It becomes the definitive archive of Ruchi Prativa Foundation.
- It remains maintainable and scalable for decades.

Success is measured by long-term institutional value rather than short-term visual appeal.

---

# Guiding Principle

Every design decision, architectural decision, content decision, and implementation decision must answer one question:

"Will this still represent Ruchi Prativa Foundation with credibility and clarity 20 years from now?"

If the answer is no, reconsider the decision.
